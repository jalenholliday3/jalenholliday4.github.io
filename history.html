Manchester United History

Manchester United is one of the most successful football clubs in world history. Founded in 1878 as Newton Heath, the club officially became Manchester United in 1902 and has since built a global legacy.

Major Trophy Record

  • English League Titles: 20 (First: 1908–09, Most Recent: 2012–13)
  • FA Cups: 12 (First: 1909–10, Most Recent: 2015–16)
  • EFL / League Cups: 6 (First: 1991–92, Most Recent: 2016–17)
  • UEFA Champions League: 3 (1967–68, 1998–99, 2007–08)
  • UEFA Europa League: 1 (2016–17)
  • FIFA Club World Cup: 1 (2008)
  • UEFA Super Cup: 1 (1991)

Major Eras of Manchester United

The Busby Era (1945–1969)

Led by Sir Matt Busby, the club rebuilt after tragedy and became European champions in 1968, becoming the first English club to win the European Cup.

The Ferguson Era (1986–2013)

Sir Alex Ferguson created the most dominant period in club history, winning 13 Premier League titles, 2 Champions Leagues, and the famous treble in 1999.

Post-Ferguson Era (2013-Present)

After Ferguson's retirement, the club has experienced rebuilding phases with multiple managers, focusing on returning to consistent European and domestic dominance.

Club Legacy Facts

  • Old Trafford is nicknamed “The Theatre of Dreams”
  • The club has over 650 million global fans
  • The 1998-99 season is known for the historic treble (League, FA Cup, UCL)
  • Manchester United is one of the most valuable football clubs in the world
